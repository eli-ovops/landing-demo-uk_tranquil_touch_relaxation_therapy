# L0 demo scope and boundaries

- Status: local-only independent concept preview; no deployment, publication, email, DM, contact, login, payment or upload.
- Shown: Hero, factual core service education, a time-bounded aggregate review signal, Plean location, two links to the existing public booking route.
- Reserved for final delivery: complete services/prices/policies, verified client imagery/logo, reviews/testimonials, credentials, social content, final business copy, legal information, maps and full contact details.
- Excluded: CMS, database, login, form, self-hosted booking, payment, gift-card checkout, analytics/tracking and customer data collection.
- Core visitor path: understand experience → click external Ovatu booking.
- Target production time: 30–60 minutes. Actual Demo Minutes: 4 (measured asset placement through final local browser capture; research/governance reading excluded).
- Closing strategy: `Specificity + Reassurance + Conversion`. The lower page adds the location and a clear explanation that live appointment details stay with the current booking service; it does not repeat a generic CTA-only close.
