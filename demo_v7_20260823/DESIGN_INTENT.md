# v7 Design Intent — Sensory mark

v7 addresses the remaining visual gap without inventing business evidence. The actual Plean room remains the one factual Hero image. A deliberately fictional `TT` sensory mark—two slow, offset orbit lines and a peach point—becomes a proprietary-looking visual signature, not a claim about the business, staff, treatment or outcome.

The Hero moves away from the common left-copy/right-photo arrangement to an immersive room image with a floating ritual panel, a large but quiet mark, and practical booking signal. Micro-detail improvements include consistent 44px actions, tactile press states, hairline rule animation, highlighted service index, and a more deliberate location pin.

Business facts remain confined to verified ASMR / Head Spa / A Bit of Both copy, dated official reviews, full address, published hours and Ovatu booking. No social, price, duration, qualification or invented testimonial is added.
