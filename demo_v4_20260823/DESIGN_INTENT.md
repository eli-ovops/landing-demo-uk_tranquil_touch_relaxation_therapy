# Design Intent — Tranquil Touch v4

## Direction

An information-first booking page with a cinematic sensory surface. The real purple-lit Plean studio is the factual arrival image. Original concept imagery adds visual tension only in the service story; it never represents the real venue, Cheryl, an actual client, a claimed device or a service outcome.

## Distinctive page rhythm

Real room / direct booking → visual service menu → a rich conceptual sensory break → review and location utilities → booking close. This avoids v3’s narrow editorial-column monotony and makes services, hours, contact, reviews and map materially prominent.

## Visual language

Deep aubergine and ink are drawn from the real room; electric peach and warm ivory form the active contrast. Oversized image crops, large practical labels, accent rules and asymmetric service blocks create a more vivid but still readable visit path. GSAP is limited to a single hero reveal and booking-button press response; reduced motion disables it.

## Authenticity and recognition

- Public studio photo and official logo are used once each, with `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED` recorded internally.
- Generated artwork is visible as non-literal concept atmosphere only; it is neither a shop, a person nor a service result.
- Unique recognisers remain the Plean address, hours, four real service names, official booking route, review evidence and actual studio image.

**Recognition test: PASS as intent.** Removing the name leaves specific service vocabulary, the real Plean setting/metadata and booking path rather than a generic spa page.
