# Risk and claims register

| Risk / claim | Status | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Cheryl is the owner/founder” | UNKNOWN | Do not say it. Public reviews/service data establish a named practitioner, not legal ownership. |
| Medical, therapeutic, mental-health or scalp-health results | PROHIBITED | Use only neutral experience descriptions supported by S2; make no treatment, diagnosis, cure, stress/anxiety or hair/scalp outcome claims. |
| Current prices, availability, opening hours, review total | VOLATILE | Recheck immediately before any concept/build; prefer external booking hand-off over static operational data. |
| Customer reviews, logo and studio/social images | RIGHTS NOT VERIFIED | Research reference only. No reuse absent specific authorization/material review. |
| “Female only” eligibility language | PUBLIC BUT SENSITIVE TO CONTEXT | Do not make it central to generic marketing; reproduce only if verified necessary and approved for the relevant page. |
| Children / guardian conditions | OPERATIONAL | Exclude from a narrow demo; it belongs in current booking/policy information. |
| Social activity | UNVERIFIED | S6–S8 links exist but contents were not retrievable in this environment. Do not invent posting cadence, audience or imagery. |
| “73 reviews” / review quality | TIME-BOUND | May be stated only after fresh, visible confirmation and without quoting reviewers; count does not equal a rating or universal satisfaction claim. |
| Direct marketing, public release or client contact | NOT AUTHORIZED | This research has no sending, login, posting, deployment or payment authority. Public email is not consent. |

No blocker above prevents a research-stage recommendation because the proposed static concept can be built from factual service/location copy and original non-factual concept imagery. Each becomes a hard recheck before any demonstration or outreach.
