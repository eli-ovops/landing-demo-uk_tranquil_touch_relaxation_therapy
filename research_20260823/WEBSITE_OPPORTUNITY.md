## Current presentation observation

The supplied same-day desktop and approximately 390px captures (S9–S10) show a good-quality branded room image, logo, Book CTA and location/contact map. The live official text layer confirms that the home’s primary content after contact details is reviews (S1). The actual offer explanations and prices sit behind the separate Book route (S2).

This is not a “bad website” finding: the current platform works as a booking tool. The demonstrable gap is that a new visitor has to enter a transactional flow to understand what ASMR massage, Head Spa or `A Bit of Both` is, who the options are for, and how the studio/location supports the choice.

## Meaningful static opportunity

**H0.5 PASS — MEANINGFUL DEMO OPPORTUNITY VERIFIED.** A one-page pre-booking introduction can honestly:

- explain the three stable core experience families using S2 descriptions;
- make the branded Plean studio, public opening details and one external booking route easy to understand;
- use aggregate, non-quoted trust only where separately revalidated (for example, “73 customer reviews on the current booking profile” as of the build check);
- hand all availability, service variants, live prices, gift-card purchase, account and payment actions back to the existing Ovatu route.

30–60-minute direction feasibility: **MEDIUM**. The page needs only a focused hero, a three-option explainer, a concise reassurance/location block and one external CTA. It must not attempt a full catalogue, dynamic appointment calendar, changing price list, gift-card checkout or customer account.
