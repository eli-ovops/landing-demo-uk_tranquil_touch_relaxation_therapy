# Research summary — Tranquil Touch Relaxation Therapy

**Conclusion: RECOMMEND** — a £600 polished static one-page Cold Demo is commercially and factually worth considering, subject to a separate authorization.

Tranquil Touch is a current, single-location Plean/Stirling ASMR massage and Head Spa business. Its official Ovatu profile provides current dated review evidence, an open public booking catalogue, a business email, location and a clear link out to booking. The booking product is functional but gives a first-time visitor little pre-booking explanation of the unusual service proposition. That makes the gap an owned-presentation/education problem rather than a software problem.

The recommended page is an education-led introduction: define three core experiences, establish local context and current trust, and direct the visitor to existing external booking. Published £35–£95 services, review volume and active booking/gift-card routes make a £600 presentation experiment reasonable to test; they are not evidence of revenue, purchase intent or expected results.

Key open boundaries: owner identity is not legally verified; social feeds were not content-reviewed; customer logo, studio images, reviews and other materials are not cleared for reuse; all volatile operational content must be rechecked. No medical/therapeutic or outcome claims are permissible.
