# v6 Design Intent — Boutique ritual editorial

## Why the v5 direction is replaced

User feedback: v5’s address was incomplete and its visual did not meet the requested standard. The poster-like oversized service type is therefore rejected, not patched. v6 moves from a graphic campaign poster to a calmer, precise boutique-service editorial.

## Customer job and distinguishing logic

The visitor needs to understand an unusual service without feeling sold to: **what it is, what to choose, where it happens, and whether booking is simple.** The real Plean studio photograph supplies place-based reassurance. The page then uses a restrained “ritual index” layout: services are arranged as distinct options, reviews establish human proof, and an accurate full address/map/hours board resolves the practical decision.

## Visual grammar

- Material palette: warm paper, inky plum, aged plum, candlelit peach and muted lavender—derived from the public studio image rather than a generic beige-spa theme.
- Type: high-contrast editorial serif only for emotional reassurance; legible sans and mono labels for operational information. No poster-stack headline.
- Hero: one dedicated actual public studio photograph. It is the sole Hero image and is allowed room to breathe rather than being hidden beneath a giant wordmark.
- Art direction: deliberate asymmetry, hairline rules, strong image crops, and a route-board map. CSS shapes supply texture; no false visual claims.

## Architecture

`real-place arrival → service ritual index → verified words → visit board with full address/map/hours → external booking`.

This intentionally avoids v5’s “poster Hero → equal three panels → art panel → metric proof → board” rhythm. The close is practical and business-specific, not a generic oversized final CTA.
