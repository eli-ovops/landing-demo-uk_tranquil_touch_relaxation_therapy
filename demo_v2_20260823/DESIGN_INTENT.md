# Design Intent — Tranquil Touch Relaxation Therapy v2

Date: 2026-08-23. Status: pre-code direction, L0 local concept only.

## The idea

This is not a generic “wellness” page. It is a **sensory-choice guide** for a visitor who may not know what an in-person ASMR massage is. The visual experience starts with Tranquil Touch’s actual lavender-lit treatment room and hand-mark logo, then moves through a tactile sequence: `listen → settle → choose → book`.

## Brand and factual anchors

- Official public hero/studio image and logo: `https://s3.amazonaws.com/assets.shrthnd.com/location/51273/images/onlineHero/large.jpg?ts=1778094367` and `https://s3.amazonaws.com/assets.shrthnd.com/location/51273/images/onlineLogo/large.png?ts=1778094367`, visible from the official BookApp profile; retrieved 2026-08-23.
- Distinctive business semantic: ASMR Massage uses gentle touch, visuals and sounds; Head Spa includes head massage, scalp exfoliation, cleansing, conditioning and blowdry; A Bit of Both combines the two. Source: `https://tranquil-touch-relaxation-therapy.book.app/book-now`, verified 2026-08-23.
- Plean location, the official external booking route and public review count are factual anchors, not generic salon filler.
- Customer authorization: **NOT VERIFIED** for all customer-recognisable materials. Use the public studio image and logo only in the minimum necessary concept positions; no claim that the business commissioned or approved this page.

## Visual language and audience

- Audience: a local first-time visitor who is intrigued but uncertain about an unfamiliar ASMR/head-spa appointment.
- Visual grammar: cinematic low-light room photography, ink-black/soft-lilac/acid-orange accents extracted from the real public profile, an all-sans typographic system with monospaced navigation labels, narrow sound-wave dividers and a deliberate contrast between dark sensory moments and precise, bright explanation.
- Page rhythm: **real studio arrival → how the experience is felt → choose by sensation, not product-card → real local reassurance → external booking hand-off**.

## Explicit rejections

- No generic AI wellness-room hero; the Hero uses the official public studio photo.
- No serif headline, equal three-card grid, isolated giant review number, or generic “ready to…” close. Display typography stays sans-serif and uses offset line rhythm rather than the prior oversized wellness-editorial treatment.
- No fake testimonials, claims of medical/therapeutic outcomes, invented process, fabricated staff, pricing, availability or booking widgets.

## Closing strategy

`Reassurance + Specificity + Conversion`: the close confirms the real Plean address, actual limited appointment days and the fact that live service details remain with Ovatu, then links there. It does not repeat a decorative CTA without new information.

## Identifiability test

**YES.** Remove the name and the page remains specifically attributable to Tranquil Touch through the official lavender studio scene/logo, the ASMR-versus-Head-Spa sensory education, the “A Bit of Both” selection logic, Plean location and Ovatu hand-off. Those elements cannot be swapped into the Wild Clover facial or Lavella hair concepts without changing the core business story.
